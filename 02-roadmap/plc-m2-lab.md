## Prioritize your Rocks and your Hard Nos
- **Rock #1:** #6, AI-generated daily check-in prompts, personalized to user history, This is the strategy. Directly builds the "we remember your recovery" differentiator and is the primary mechanism expected to move KR1. Caveat: the word "daily" needs to be re-scoped to contextual/need-triggered before build as literally worded this risks becoming the streak mechanic we ruled out. Route through the clinical veto before shipping.
- **Rock #2:** #4, Fix notification system (wrong times, timezone bug), Directly protects the counter-metric (notification complaint/opt-out rate) named in the management system. A mistimed notification is exactly the kind of friction that erodes KR3 sentiment and undermines trust in the re-entry experience before the personalization work even ships. Cheap, high-leverage, do first.
- **Rock #3:** #10, Close WCAG 2.1 accessibility gap, Exception to normal scoring: this doesn't move the north star directly, but it's a compliance mandate, not a strategic bet ignoring it is a legal exposure, not a prioritization choice. Do now regardless of OKR score; don't let it compete with strategic Rocks for the "is this worth it" conversation.
- **❌ Hard No #1:** #2, Daily streak feature, Directly violates the stated hard no on compulsion mechanics. Would likely lift raw engagement while actively damaging KR3 (sentiment) and contaminating KR1 with exactly the gamed signal the metric was designed to exclude. Low effort, which is precisely why it's dangerous it's the easiest wrong answer available.
- **❌ Hard No #2:** #3, Social layer: share progress with friends, This is the hard no, named explicitly. Visibility is the opposite of what the highest-value user wants; this risks the trust architecture the entire differentiator depends on.
- **❌ Hard No #3:** #5, Premium tier with therapist-matching, Conflicts with the explicit constraint against clinical repositioning. This is the clearest "loudest source ≠ most strategic" case in the backlog it carries the most organizational authority and the weakest strategic fit. Don't quietly build around this; take it back to the CEO as a direct conversation about the constraint, not a backlog item to prioritize or silently drop.
